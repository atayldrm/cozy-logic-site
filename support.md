# Cozy Logic Support

Cozy Logic is a collection of logic puzzles set in a cozy workshop. It works offline, requires no account and contains no ads.

## The games

**Number Forge:** Combine number stones using arithmetic to reach the target. Follow the campaign or play a fresh puzzle. Fractions remain exact, and Undo lets you revisit a move.

**Rune Code:** Discover a hidden sequence of runes. Exact feedback counts symbols in the correct position; Misplaced feedback counts correct symbols in other positions.

**Lantern Grid:** Fill the notebook so each row and column contains every number once. The numbers in each cage must satisfy its arithmetic clue. Use pencil marks to explore possibilities.

**Daily Challenges:** Open the daily letter from the workshop for one puzzle from each game. You can return to recent letters and resume your saved daily puzzles. Daily progress is separate from normal game saves.

## Saves and offline play

Puzzles, campaign and Daily progress, keepsakes, statistics and settings are saved locally on your device. No internet connection is required to play. There is no account-based sync between devices.

Deleting the app or confirming a local-data reset can remove your saves. Do not reset or reinstall the app as a first troubleshooting step if you want to preserve progress. Device backups depend on your system settings.

## Language and game help

Open **Settings** from the workshop, then choose **Language**. Select English, Turkish, French, German, Russian, Italian or Simplified Chinese, or choose **System default**. An explicit language choice is saved on your device.

For game rules, open a game and select **How to play** from its help control or menu. Each game includes an explanation of its core rules. Lantern Grid help also explains cage operations and pencil marks. Labels are translated when you change the app language.

## Reporting a problem

Email [info.cozy.logic@gmail.com](mailto:info.cozy.logic@gmail.com). To help us understand the problem, include:

- App version and build number from **Settings → About**.
- Device model and iOS version.
- The game or screen affected, and the selected language.
- Steps to reproduce the issue, what you expected and what happened.
- For Daily issues, the daily letter's date and whether the device date or time zone changed.
- Whether the issue occurs after reopening the app, and any relevant accessibility settings.
- A screenshot if helpful, after removing personal information.

Do not send passwords, payment details, private keys or other sensitive information. You do not need to send your local database or device backup.
