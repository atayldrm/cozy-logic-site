# Cozy Logic Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

Cozy Logic is an offline-first puzzle game owned by Ata Yıldırım. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## No account or developer data collection

You do not need an account to use Cozy Logic. The app has no ads, analytics, tracking or backend. We do not intentionally collect personal data through the app, and the app does not send your game data to the developer.

We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

## Information stored on your device

Cozy Logic stores information locally so you can continue playing and keep your preferences. This includes:

- Puzzle saves and game progress.
- Number Forge campaign progress.
- Daily Challenge progress and recent daily letters.
- Keepsakes and game statistics.
- Settings, including language, accessibility, sound and haptic preferences.

This information is used on your device. Cozy Logic does not provide a developer-operated cloud save service.

## Removing local data

You can remove local game data using the available reset options in Settings or by deleting the app. On iOS, offloading the app retains its documents and data; choose **Delete App** to remove its local data.

Device backups may retain a copy depending on your system settings. Restoring a backup may restore app data. Backup management is handled by your device's platform services.

## Apple platform services

Apple services, including the App Store, TestFlight, device backups and diagnostics, may process information under Apple's own privacy policies. These services are separate from Cozy Logic's local game data handling. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Policy updates

If the app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated. The date above identifies the latest revision.

## Privacy questions

For privacy questions, email [info.cozy.logic@gmail.com](mailto:info.cozy.logic@gmail.com).

If you choose to contact support, your email address and the information you send are used to respond to your request. This is separate from the app's local data handling. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. See the [Support page](support.html) for guidance.
